Privacy Policy for .lara

Last updated: July 28, 2026

.lara is a Discord community economy, achievement, and progression bot.

Data processed from Discord

When required to provide its features, the bot processes Discord user IDs, role IDs, current guild role memberships, and guild join timestamps. The bot does not process or store message content, individual presence status, activities, email addresses, passwords, or Discord authentication tokens.

Data stored

The bot stores Discord user IDs and bot-specific state linked to those IDs, including currency balances, scores, achievements, collectible items, and bot-level permissions. It also stores configured Discord role IDs and their score values. Current guild member lists, role memberships, and join timestamps are retrieved when a command or scheduled task requires them and are not stored as a separate member directory.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide inventories, achievements, rankings, role-based score calculation, ascension, staff-authorized reward management, role-to-achievement transfer, scheduled role rewards, and membership-age role automation.

Sharing and sale

Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties except where required by law or as necessary to operate the hosting infrastructure.

Retention and deletion

Stored bot data is retained while it is needed to operate the community progression system. Users may request access to or deletion of their stored bot data by contacting nepupa77@gmail.com. The requester may be asked to verify control of the relevant Discord account.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect stored data. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact nepupa77@gmail.com.
